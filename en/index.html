What you eat, what you weigh and how you train, in one place.

Persevia is an Android app that helps you keep track of your food and your progress without fuss, with recipes and workouts shared by the community.

  • Food diaryCalories and macros, with food entries reviewed by the community.
  • Recipes and pantryPlan your week, track what you have and build your shopping list.
  • Workouts and progressRoutines, personal records, weight and measurements, with your steps from Health Connect.
  • Medicine cabinetMedication reminders and a heads-up when you're running low.

It works without an account. If you sign in, your data is backed up to the cloud and synced across devices.

Questions or suggestions? Write to support@persevia.app.
