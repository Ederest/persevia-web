1. What Persevia is

Persevia is an app for keeping track of your food, weight, workouts and medication, with a social side where people share recipes, workouts and experiences. It is provided by Alexandre Espinosa Gálvez, Sant Celoni (Barcelona), Spain. Contact: support@persevia.app.

By using the app or creating an account you accept these terms. If you don't agree, don't use it.

2. Persevia does not give medical advice

Persevia is a tracking tool, not a medical device. It doesn't diagnose, treat or recommend treatments, and it doesn't replace a professional.

  • The numbers are estimates. Daily energy use is calculated with standard population formulas (Mifflin-St Jeor and equivalents), which can be off by several hundred kilocalories for a given person. Food entries come from public databases and community corrections and may contain errors.
  • Your medication is decided with your doctor. The medicine cabinet and reminders only reflect the schedule you enter. They don't check doses or interactions, and a reminder may fail to go off (phone off, battery saving, permissions). Don't rely on the app alone for anything critical.
  • See a professional before making major changes to your diet or activity, especially if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, have a chronic condition, take medication affected by what you eat, are under 18 or over 65, or have or have had an eating disorder.
  • Aggressive deficits. The app allows them because some people use them under supervision, and it warns you when you choose one. The warning is meant seriously.

Counting calories helps many people and harms others. If you notice the app pushing you to eat less than you should or to punish yourself over a number, stop using it and talk to someone. If you are in crisis, contact your local emergency number.
